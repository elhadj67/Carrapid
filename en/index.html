The stops

9 sights, one day

1

Independence Square

The heart of the Plateau district, with its fountain and modernist buildings lined with tree-shaded walkways. The starting point of the tour.

230 min stop

Kermel Market

Under the round 1910 market hall and its clock arch: flowers, spices, fabrics and wax prints. Sandaga market is just around the corner.

3

Cathedral of African Remembrance

Its twin towers and ochre dome overlook Boulevard de la République. Built in memory of African soldiers, it is the Plateau's great Christian landmark.

4

Dakar Railway Station

The Senegal Railways station (1914), restored with its arcades and clock. Terminus of the TER commuter train.

530 min stop

Museum of Black Civilizations

Its great rotunda is inspired by traditional impluvium houses: one of the largest museums in Africa.

6

Grand Mosque of Dakar

Its 67-metre minaret, decorated with green tiles, towers over the Medina.

720 min stop

Corniche Ouest — Millennium Monument

The grand coastal avenue along the Atlantic, with its palm trees and promenades — and a stop at the Millennium Monument, facing the ocean.

830 min stop

African Renaissance Monument

52 metres of bronze rising from the Mamelles hills: the tallest statue in Africa.

920 min stop

Mamelles Lighthouse

Perched on its volcanic hill: a 360° panorama, then on to Pointe des Almadies, the westernmost tip of Africa.
